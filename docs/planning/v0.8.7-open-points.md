- **Shadow support — COMPLETE (v6.1, 2026-06-11).** The full history
  below is archaeology; the placement bug was solved in v6 (2026-05-22)
  and the pipeline completed in v6.1. Current architecture and tuning
  knobs: `docs/planning/shadows_state.md`. Acceptance criteria (all six
  met): `docs/planning/shadow_goal.md`. PR to `main`: #11.

  Short version of the fix: `SHADOWTYPE_TEXTURE_MODULATIVE` + custom
  GLSL caster/receiver, with `ShadowMapper` (an `Ogre::ShadowTextureListener`)
  pushing the shadow camera's `proj × view` into the receiver material
  from C++ each frame — bypassing Ogre 14's broken
  `texture_worldviewproj_matrix` auto-param on macOS arm64 GL3+ core,
  which was the root cause of the displaced-shadow bug documented in the
  archaeology below. v6.1 added a world-anchored fixed shadow camera,
  `PF_FLOAT32_R` depth, and 3×3 PCF.

  <details><summary>Archaeology: the placement-bug investigation (v3–v5, 2026-05-18/19)</summary>

  **What worked by v5:** real dynamic shadow maps rendered. Two-layer
  Ogre 14 issue resolved: (1)
  `SceneManager::setShadowTextureCasterMaterial(MaterialPtr)` required
  in addition to `Technique::setShadowCasterMaterial`; (2) a custom
  GLSL receiver wired via `setShadowTextureReceiverMaterial` (the
  fixed-function `Ogre/TextureShadowReceiver` breaks the modulating
  pass on GL3+ core). Both materials tag their technique with
  `scheme ShaderGeneratorDefaultScheme`.

  **What was broken:** shadow placement offset from casters. The
  receiver shader's `texture_viewproj_matrix * vertex_object_space`
  projected local coords as world positions. `texture_worldviewproj_matrix`
  and explicit `world * tex_viewproj * vertex` made shadows vanish
  (sampled where the caster didn't write); Y-flip variants produced a
  blob in the wrong corner. Resolved in v6 by pushing the matrix from
  C++ via `ShadowMapper` instead of relying on the auto-param.

  </details>

## Raycast optimization outcome (2026-07-07)

Branch `feat/raycast-optimization` (SHA `53a0259`), five commits (incl. the baseline doc commit): `377a0d6`
(btVector3 `rayTest` overload), `ed582ca` (proximity rays clamped to running
min, pruning BVH traversal), `455a41b` (hoisted `Pose::operator<<`
composition out of `GenericProximitySensor`'s per-ray loop — closes the
finding above), `53a0259` (LDR ray moved onto the same btVector3 overload).

- **`braitenberg_zoo.xml`** (20k iterations, many robots/sensors): median
  wall-clock **2.209s → 1.997s, ~9.6%** faster (~9,054 → ~10,015 steps/s).
  Just under the ≥10% target set in the optimization plan.
- **`braitenberg.xml`** (100k iterations, single robot): median wall-clock
  2.163s → 2.139s, ~1.1% — within run-to-run noise, as expected since this
  scenario has far fewer proximity/LDR rays per step than the zoo.
- Profile spot-check (400k-iteration zoo run, 5s `sample`) confirms
  `Pose::operator<<` dropped from 43 to 5 samples but Bullet's own
  `rayTestInternal`/narrowphase frames (`btDbvt::rayTestInternal`,
  `btSubsimplexConvexCast::calcTimeOfImpact`, `btVoronoiSimplexSolver::*`)
  did not shrink — none of the five commits touch Bullet's internal
  algorithms, only conversions and redundant work around the calls into
  it. Raycasting/narrowphase itself remains the dominant cost; further
  gains would need a more invasive approach (fewer rays per step, spatial
  batching, or a custom broadphase).
- **Result-preserving**: `braitenberg_logging.xml` and `hexapod_logging.xml`
  regression gates are bit-exact (empty diff) at every task and at the
  final SHA. A dedicated LDR A/B gate (`braitenberg_light_source.xml`)
  also confirmed byte-identical output for Task 5.
- Full measurements, per-task deltas, and the before/after profile table:
  `docs/planning/raycast-baseline.md`.

- **Metal render system: NO-GO (spike 2026-07-07).** Three independent
  blockers: no RTSS MSL writer (fork + upstream), Ogre's own bundled
  `DefaultShaders.metal` fails MSL compilation (GLSL-flavored source,
  never translated), and `PF_FLOAT32_R` shadow textures unsupported in
  `MetalTextureBuffer::upload`. SDL2/NSWindow glue confirmed working.
  Full evidence: `docs/planning/metal-spike-findings.md`. Plan shelved;
  re-evaluate on upstream RTSS-Metal support or an actual GL break.
